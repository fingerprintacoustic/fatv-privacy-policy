FA TV Privacy Policy & Data Deletion Guide

Last Updated: June 1, 2026

Privacy Policy

Overview

FA TV ("we", "us", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the FA TV IPTV streaming application.

Data We Collect

FA TV collects minimal data, all stored locally on your device only:

Data Type Purpose Storage Location
Favorite Channels Quick access to preferred channels Local device storage
Watch History Remember recently watched channels Local device storage
Cached Playlists Offline access to playlists Local device storage
Cached EPG Data Offline access to program guides Local device storage
Notification Preferences Program reminder settings Local device storage
App Settings User preferences Local device storage

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Browsing history or viewing habits
  • Payment information
  • Biometric data
  • Contact list or social media data

Data Sharing

We do NOT share your data with any third parties. All data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or external companies.

External Content

FA TV streams content from third-party IPTV sources and user-added playlists. When you stream content:

  • Your device connects directly to the stream source.
  • We do not intercept, monitor, or log your streaming activity.
  • Stream providers may collect their own usage data according to their privacy policies.

Data Security

  • All data is stored locally on your Android device.
  • Data in transit uses HTTPS encryption when available.
  • No data is sent to FA TV servers.
  • Your device's built-in security protects your data.

Data Retention

  • Data remains on your device until deleted.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all associated data.
  • Clearing app data removes all stored information.

How to Delete Your Data

Method 1: Clear App Data

  1. Open Settings on your Android device
  2. Navigate to Apps
  3. Select FA TV
  4. Tap Storage
  5. Select Clear Cache
  6. Select Clear Data
  7. Confirm deletion

Method 2: Uninstall the App

  1. Long-press the FA TV app icon
  2. Select Uninstall
  3. Confirm removal

What Gets Deleted

  • Favorite channels
  • Watch history
  • Cached playlists
  • Cached EPG data
  • Notification preferences
  • App settings

Your Rights

  • Access stored data within the app
  • Delete all data at any time
  • Use the app without creating an account
  • Opt out of notifications

Children's Privacy

FA TV is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove such information.
