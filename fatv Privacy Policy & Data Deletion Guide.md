# fatv Privacy Policy & Data Deletion Guide

**Last Updated:** June 1, 2026

## Privacy Policy

### Overview

fatv ("we," "us," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the fatv IPTV streaming application.

### Data We Collect

fatv collects minimal data, all stored **locally on your device only**:

| Data Type | Purpose | Storage Location |
|-----------|---------|------------------|
| **Favorite Channels** | Quick access to preferred channels | Local device storage |
| **Watch History** | Remember recently watched channels | Local device storage |
| **Cached Playlists** | Offline access to M3U playlists | Local device storage |
| **Cached EPG Data** | Offline access to program guides | Local device storage |
| **Notification Preferences** | Program reminder settings | Local device storage |
| **App Settings** | User preferences (theme, language, etc.) | Local device storage |

### Data We Do NOT Collect

- ❌ Personal information (name, email, phone number)
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- ❌ Browsing history or viewing habits
- ❌ Payment information (app is free, no purchases)
- ❌ Biometric data
- ❌ Contact list or social media data

### Data Sharing

**We do NOT share your data with any third parties.** All data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or external companies.

### External Content

fatv streams content from third-party IPTV sources (IPTV-Org and user-added playlists). When you stream content:
- Your device connects directly to the stream source
- We do not intercept, monitor, or log your streaming activity
- Stream providers may collect their own usage data per their privacy policies

### Data Security

- All data is stored locally on your Android device
- Data in transit (fetching playlists/EPG) uses HTTPS encryption
- No data is sent to fatv servers
- Your device's built-in security protects your data

### Data Retention

- Data persists on your device until you manually delete it
- Uninstalling the app removes all associated data
- Clearing app cache/data removes all stored information
- No automatic deletion occurs

## How to Delete Your Data

### Method 1: Clear App Data (Recommended)

This completely removes all fatv data from your device:

1. Open **Settings** on your Android device
2. Navigate to **Apps** (or **Application Manager**)
3. Find and tap **fatv**
4. Tap **Storage**
5. Select **Clear Cache** (removes cached playlists/EPG)
6. Select **Clear Data** (removes favorites, watch history, settings)
7. Confirm deletion
8. All fatv data is now permanently removed

### Method 2: Uninstall the App

Uninstalling fatv automatically removes all associated data:

1. Long-press the **fatv** app icon on your home screen
2. Select **Uninstall**
3. Confirm uninstallation
4. All data is permanently deleted

### Method 3: Clear Individual Cache (Partial)

To remove only offline playlists and EPG cache while keeping favorites:

1. Open **fatv**
2. Navigate to the **Cache** tab
3. Tap **Clear Cache**
4. Confirm deletion
5. Offline data is removed; favorites and watch history remain

### What Gets Deleted

When you clear data, the following are permanently removed:

| Data | Deleted? |
|------|----------|
| Favorite channels list | ✅ Yes |
| Watch history | ✅ Yes |
| Cached playlists | ✅ Yes |
| Cached EPG data | ✅ Yes |
| Notification preferences | ✅ Yes |
| App settings | ✅ Yes |

### What Is NOT Deleted

- ✅ Channels still available from IPTV-Org (not affected by app deletion)
- ✅ Your device's other apps and data (only fatv data is removed)
